# Fact-Verification Checklist

Use this checklist for every material factual claim. Mark each item `pass`, `fail`, or `not applicable`, and record the source IDs and reviewer.

## Identity and names

- Confirm every person's, organization’s, product’s, project’s, location’s, and dataset’s name against an authoritative source.
- Check aliases, spelling, transliteration, role, affiliation, and identity collisions.
- Record the source and the date checked; do not infer identity from an unverified username or image.

## Dates and chronology

- Confirm publication, event, effective, update, and access dates separately.
- Check timezone, calendar, reporting period, and whether the source is current for the task's freshness window.
- Verify that the described sequence is supported and does not confuse announcement, occurrence, and implementation dates.

## Numerical values and units

- Recalculate arithmetic, percentages, totals, rates, ranges, and conversions independently.
- Confirm units, currency, scale, denominator, sample size, significant figures, rounding, and geographic or fiscal scope.
- Check that values are comparable across sources and periods; disclose any normalization or estimate.

## Claims and causal language

- Decompose compound claims into independently verifiable statements.
- Confirm that the cited source directly supports the wording, scope, certainty, and time period.
- Distinguish observation, correlation, causation, forecast, opinion, and interpretation.
- Label unsupported, disputed, stale, inaccessible, or unresolved claims instead of filling gaps.

## Quotations and attribution

- Verify the exact wording, speaker or author, context, date, and source location.
- Preserve qualifiers and surrounding context; do not convert a paraphrase into a quotation.
- Use quotation marks only for text verified against the source, and flag translations or transcript uncertainty.

## Final review gate

- Every material claim has a source ID or is explicitly marked unresolved.
- Primary evidence, secondary reporting, interpretation, and uncertainty are separated.
- Publication date, access date, URL or safe private identifier, and evidence scope are recorded.
- Current-source and corroboration requirements are satisfied for the risk tier.
- No credentials, cookies, authorization headers, private personal data, or unredacted private content appear in the record.
- A second reviewer or independent verification pass completed the checklist.
