## Findings

### Primary evidence

State only what the primary sources directly support. Cite claims inline with numbered reference links such as [1].

### Secondary reporting

Identify the reporting source and explain whether it corroborates, contextualizes, or conflicts with primary evidence.

### Interpretation

Separate analysis or inference from source statements. Describe the reasoning and assumptions.

### Unresolved uncertainty

List missing, stale, contradictory, inaccessible, or out-of-scope evidence. State what would resolve each uncertainty.

## Limitations and safety

Record scope limitations, access restrictions, possible conflicts of interest, and any human-review requirement. Do not include credentials, cookies, authorization headers, private personal information, or unredacted private content.
