## Findings

### Primary evidence

Present claims directly supported by authoritative primary sources. Use inline numbered citations such as [1].

### Secondary reporting

Summarize secondary sources separately and explain whether they corroborate, contextualize, or conflict with primary evidence.

### Interpretation

Explain analysis, comparisons, inferences, assumptions, and implications separately from source statements. Identify where judgment is required.

### Unresolved uncertainty

List stale, missing, inaccessible, contradictory, or out-of-scope evidence and state what additional evidence would resolve each issue.

## Limitations

Describe source coverage, recency, methodology, access restrictions, measurement limits, possible bias, generalization limits, and any human-review requirement. Do not include secrets, cookies, authorization headers, or private personal information.

## Conclusion and next actions

State only conclusions supported by the evidence and list bounded follow-up actions, owners, or review gates. Consequential external actions require separate explicit authorization.
